How these numbers are counted

  • Every message to and from Sabi in the pilot is logged on the server; these are totals across all shops, with no names or chats.
  • Entries are double-entry records in each trader's books, each checked by code before it was saved.
  • Reply time is the median time from a message arriving to Sabi's answer, including the AI model.
  • Errors are replies where something failed on Sabi's side. Thumbs down are replies a trader flagged as wrong.
  • Quotes appear only with the trader's permission.
