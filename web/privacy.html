Sabi Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 November 2026

Sabi is a WhatsApp assistant that keeps the books for small shops and answers their customers. This page says what Sabi collects, why, who processes it, and how to have it deleted. Contact: akomodesegbeisrael@gmail.com.

What Sabi collects

  • From shop owners: WhatsApp number and profile name; the messages, voice notes and photos sent to Sabi; the sales, purchases, debts, stock, prices, customers and suppliers recorded from them; shop details and settings.
  • From a shop's customers: WhatsApp number and profile name, the messages and photos they send, their orders and payment claims, and the products they asked about.

Why

Only to run the service: to record the shop's books, answer the owner and her customers, send summaries and reminders the owner approved, and show the owner her records. Sabi does not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

Who processes it

  • Meta (WhatsApp Cloud API) delivers messages; Meta Model API and Baseten run the AI models that read messages, voice notes and photos.
  • Render hosts the app; Supabase stores the books, messages and saved files.

How long it is kept

A shop's records are kept while the shop uses Sabi, because they are its books. Voice notes and photos are kept as the evidence behind each entry. Everything is deleted on request (below).

Deleting your data

Email akomodesegbeisrael@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Sabi data" and the WhatsApp number you used. A shop owner's request deletes the shop's books, messages, voice notes and photos; a customer's request deletes their messages, photos and contact details from the shop's records. Deletion is done within 30 days, and you are told when it is done.

Security

Data is sent over HTTPS. The owner's books are only shown to someone with her app key, and her voice notes and photos are never at a public address.
